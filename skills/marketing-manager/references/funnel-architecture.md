# Carbon Funnel & Retargeting Architecture
### From First Impression to Lifetime Client

Carbon's customer journey is structured across four stages — Awareness, Consideration, Conversion, and Loyalty. Each stage has a distinct emotional objective, content register, and retargeting logic. The funnel is never price-led. At every stage, Carbon earns attention through beauty, trust, and relevance — never through discount or urgency.

| Stage | Name | Objective | Primary Trigger |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TOFU** | Aspirational Validation | Create emotional desire and brand recognition | Belonging / Aspiration |
| **MOFU** | Product Relatability | Move desire toward a specific collection or occasion | Connoisseurship / Status |
| **BOFU** | Decision & Conversion | Convert interest into consultation or purchase | Trust / Exclusivity |
| **POST-PURCHASE** | Loyalty & Advocacy | Transform a buyer into a lifetime client and advocate | Belonging / Legacy |

---

## 1. TOFU — Aspirational Validation

- **Goal:** Reach premium audiences through lifestyle and emotional connection.
- **Content:** TTL imagery showing the "Carbon Lifestyle" — Belonging, beauty, the world Carbon inhabits.
- **Trigger:** First visit or interaction with social content.

### Additions — TOFU
- **Audience Segmentation:** Do not treat TOFU as one audience. Segment from the start:
  - **Cold premium:** Lookalike audiences built from Heritage Circle client data and in-store purchase history.
  - **Occasion-intent:** Users showing wedding, anniversary, or festive season intent signals.
  - **Geography-first:** City-specific TOFU — Noida sees status and modernity; Ahmedabad sees legacy and craft.
- **Content Format Guidance:** 
  - **Instagram / Meta:** 15–30 second atmospheric Reels. No product specs. No CTAs that sound transactional. Mood-first.
  - **YouTube pre-roll:** Brand film cuts. The story of Carbon, the story of lab-grown innovation. 60–90 seconds.
  - **Pinterest / Display:** Editorial imagery. Jewellery in lifestyle context, not on white backgrounds.
- **TOFU Voice Rule:** At this stage, Carbon whispers. The brand does not shout. No CTAs with "Buy", "Shop", or "Book". Acceptable CTAs: "Discover the World of Carbon", "Enter", "Explore".
- **What TOFU Success Looks Like:** 3-second video views, saves, profile visits, follower growth from target demographics. Not clicks to website. Not conversions. Patience here is a brand discipline.

## 2. MOFU — Product Relatability

- **Goal:** Drive interest in specific collections — Hues, Polki, Solitaires.
- **Content:** Educational content on craftsmanship, design-led USP, and lab-grown innovation.
- **Activity:** In-store BTL activities — Mixology evenings, trunk show previews.

### Additions — MOFU
- **Collection-Level MOFU Logic:** Each collection has its own MOFU narrative. Match the collection to the correct ICP before choosing content:

| Collection | MOFU Narrative | Target ICP | Best Format |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hues (Coloured Stone)** | Colour as emotion — the stone's story | Self-Purchase Woman, Status Investor | Reel series, Stone Stories event |
| **Polki** | The craft of the ancient, reborn in modern form | Legacy Bride, Occasion Celebrant | Long-form video, Heirloom Masterclass |
| **Solitaires** | The intelligent diamond — science meets emotion | Status Investor, Legacy Bride | Carousel, Design Your Own session |
| **Everyday Fine** | Part of who you are — worn daily, loved always | Self-Purchase Woman | Styling Reels, editorial photography |
| **Men's** | Strength through restraint — designed for the discerning man | Status Investor (male), gifting buyers | Short film, private preview |

- **MOFU Retargeting Entry Point:** A user enters MOFU retargeting after: (a) watching 50%+ of a TOFU video, (b) saving a TOFU post, or (c) visiting the Carbon website for the first time without taking action.
- **Content Format Shift — TOFU to MOFU:** 
  - Video (TOFU) → Image Carousel or Reel with voice-over (MOFU)
  - Atmospheric (TOFU) → Collection-specific (MOFU)
  - Brand story (TOFU) → Craftsmanship and design story (MOFU)
- **MOFU BTL Integration:** When a user has engaged with MOFU content AND lives within 10km of a store, trigger an event invitation as the next touchpoint — not another ad. The invitation is the conversion mechanism, not a hard sell.
- **MOFU Voice Rule:** Carbon becomes more specific here but never clinical. "This is how a Hues emerald is set" — not "Our emeralds have X-grade clarity". Emotion remains the vehicle. Knowledge is the passenger.

## 3. BOFU — Decision & Conversion

- **Goal:** Decision and conversion.
- **Content:** Trust-building testimonials, certification details, and "Book an Appointment" CTAs.
- **Retargeting Rule:** If a user visits the "Hues" page but does not book, retarget with a specialised "Hues Location Event" invite for their nearest branch.

### Additions — BOFU
- **Extended Retargeting Rules by Behaviour:** 

| User Behaviour | Retargeting Response | Format | Timing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Visited collection page, no action | Location-specific event invite for that collection | Personal Invite creative | Within 48 hours |
| Visited 'Book an Appointment' page, did not complete | WhatsApp message from store team: 'We noticed you were looking at X — shall we reserve a private viewing?' | WhatsApp + retargeting ad | Within 24 hours |
| Engaged with 3+ MOFU posts but no site visit | Behind-the-craft content — the making of the collection they engaged with | Reel or carousel | Day 3–5 |
| Visited homepage only | Carbon brand film — deepen the world before pushing collection | Video ad | Day 2–4 |
| Abandoned 'Design Your Own' enquiry | Personal outreach from design team via WhatsApp | WhatsApp only — no ad | Within 12 hours |

- **Trust Signals at BOFU — Content Checklist:** 
  - **IGI certification** — show the certificate, not just mention it
  - **BIS hallmark** — explain what it means in plain Carbon voice
  - **100% lifetime exchange + 90% buyback** — frame as Carbon's commitment, not a safety net
  - **Client testimonials** — real voices, unscripted, specific occasions
  - **Artisan stories** — who made this piece and what they brought to it
- **BOFU CTA Hierarchy:** CTAs at this stage become more direct but never aggressive. In order of preference:
  1. Book a Private Viewing
  2. Speak to Our Design Concierge
  3. Visit Us in [City]
  4. Explore the [Collection] — only if 1–3 have already been served
- **BOFU Voice Rule:** Warmth increases here. Carbon is not closing a sale — it is inviting the client into a relationship. The language of BOFU is: "We would love to show you this in person." Never: "Limited time", "Act now", "Don't miss out."

## 4. Retargeting Logic

- **Recency:** 0–7 days (High intensity), 8–30 days (Nurturing).
- **Frequency:** Limit to 3–5 touchpoints to maintain premium feel.
- **Format:** Shift from Video (TOFU) to Image Carousel (MOFU) to Personal Invite (BOFU).

### Additions — Retargeting Architecture
- **Full Recency & Intensity Map:** 

| Days Since Interaction | Stage | Intensity | Touchpoints Allowed | Format |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 0–3 days | BOFU | High | 2 max | Personal Invite or WhatsApp |
| 4–7 days | BOFU | High | 1 per 2 days | Testimonial or trust-signal creative |
| 8–14 days | MOFU | Medium | 1 per 3 days | Collection carousel or craft video |
| 15–30 days | MOFU–TOFU | Nurturing | 1 per 5 days | Lifestyle or brand story content |
| 31–60 days | TOFU re-entry | Low | 1 per week | New collection or seasonal moment |
| 60+ days | Dormant | Suppress | 0 | Remove from active retargeting |

- **Frequency Cap by Platform:** 
  - **Meta (Instagram + Facebook):** Maximum 3 impressions per user per 7 days across all Carbon ad sets
  - **Google Display:** Maximum 2 impressions per user per day
  - **YouTube:** Maximum 1 pre-roll per user per 3 days
  - **WhatsApp:** Never automated at scale — only personalised, store-team-initiated messages
- **Suppression Rules — Who Carbon Never Retargets:** 
  - Anyone who has purchased in the last 30 days — move to post-purchase nurture, not retargeting
  - Anyone who has explicitly declined contact
  - Anyone who has visited 10+ times without converting — flag for personal WhatsApp outreach instead of continued ad spend
- **Cross-Channel Sequencing Rule:** A user should never receive the same creative format twice in a row. If they saw a video last, serve a carousel next. If they received a WhatsApp, pause ads for 3 days. The experience must feel curated, not algorithmic.

## 5. Post-Purchase — Loyalty & Advocacy

- **Goal:** Transform every buyer into a lifetime client and a quiet advocate for Carbon.
- **Trigger:** Purchase confirmed — whether in-store or via concierge.

### Immediate Post-Purchase (Day 0–7)
- **Personalised thank-you message** from the store director — handwritten note with the piece, or WhatsApp from the consultant who served them.
- **Care guide** for the specific piece purchased.
- **IGI certificate delivery** — digital copy within 24 hours.
- **Heritage Circle enrolment invitation** — offered as a privilege.

### Nurture Phase (Day 8–90)
- **Engagement Content:** If they bought a Hues emerald, send the Hues craft story. If they bought bridal, send the care-and-styling guide for the wedding day.
- **Occasion Reminder:** Set a calendar reminder to reach out before that date next year.
- **No retargeting ads in this window.**

### Advocacy Activation (Day 90+)
- **Invite to the Carbon Collective (Heritage Circle annual evening)** if spend qualifies.
- **Testimonial Request:** We would love to know how the piece has been received.
- **Referral:** Incentivise with access: early preview of a new collection, a private design consultation.

- **Post-Purchase Voice Rule:** The relationship has shifted. Carbon is sustaining a connection. Language becomes more personal, more direct, more like a trusted friend.
