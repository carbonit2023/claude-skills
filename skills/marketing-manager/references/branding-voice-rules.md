# Carbon Branding & Voice Rules

This document ensures that every piece of content generated by any skill remains within the premium "Jewel of Emotion" boundary.

## 1. The Core Tone
- **Premium:** Elegant but not cold.
- **Warm:** Emotionally intelligent and empathetic.
- **Aspirational:** Makes the customer feel like they are "arriving" at a new standard of luxury.

### 1A. Sentence Rhythm & Cadence
Carbon's written voice has a deliberate architecture. Short declarative lines are used to open with impact — they command attention. Flowing, descriptive prose follows to deepen desire and paint the sensory world of the piece.
- **Structural principle:** Open with impact. Sustain with beauty.
- **Lead sentences:** Short. Decisive. Maximum 8–10 words.
- **Body copy:** Richer and more flowing — allow the reader to inhabit the feeling of the jewellery.
- **Closing lines:** Return to brevity. Leave the reader with a feeling, not a fact.

Example rhythm: 
> "Some moments deserve to be worn. The Lumière Solitaire is crafted for exactly this — a single diamond, suspended in hand-set platinum, carrying the weight of everything unsaid. Wear it. Remember it."

## 2. Word Choice — The Dictionary

### ✅ Use — Elevated Language
- Craftsmanship / Heirloom
- Brilliance / Radiance / Maison
- Design-led / Iconic / Timeless
- **Whisper / Luminous / Weightless** — for describing delicate forms and light-play on stones.
- **Considered / Intentional** — for design narrative and craftsmanship stories.
- **Belonging / Becoming** — for emotional brand storytelling.

### ❌ Avoid — Mass-Market Language
- Cheap / Affordable
- Imitation / Artificial (Use "Lab-Grown Innovation")
- Sale / Discount (Use "Selective Value" or "Celebratory Offer")
- Best Deal / Lowest Price
- Stunning / Amazing / Gorgeous — overused; replace with precise emotional language.
- Perfect for / Great for — replaces with "made for the woman who…"
- Buy Now / Shop Now — replace with "Explore", "Discover", "Enter the World of Carbon".

## 3. Communication Style
- **Headline Strategy:** Focus on the emotion of the wearer, not just the details of the stone.
- **CTA Strategy:** Use soft luxury verbs: *Explore*, *Discover*, *Discover the Collection*, *Enter the World of Carbon*.
- **Location Nuance:** 
  - *Ahmedabad:* "Legacy" and "Trust."
  - *Noida / Gurugram:* "Modernity" and "Status."
  - *Mumbai:* "Individuality" and "Connoisseurship."
  - *Jamnagar:* "Community" and "Occasion."

## 4. What Carbon Never Does
- **Never lead with price**, even when a price point is the message. Let the piece speak first.
- **Never compare to competitors** by name or implication. Carbon exists in its own world.
- **Never use superlatives** as shortcuts: "best," "most," "number one" are lazy. Use precise, evocative language instead.
- **Never be transactional** in creative copy. Features and specifications belong in product descriptions — not in campaign writing.
- **Never over-explain the lab-grown story.** Frame it as innovation and consciousness, not as a disclaimer. *"Born in a laboratory. Worn for a lifetime."*
- **Never use exclamation marks** in maison-level copy. Excitement is carried by language, not punctuation.

## 5. Voice by Category
- **Bridal:** Reverent, intimate, celebratory. Focus on the bride's inner world.
- **Statement & Occasion:** Bold, knowing, quietly theatrical. The feeling of being seen on one's own terms.
- **Everyday Fine:** Quiet, personal, deeply considered. How a piece becomes part of who she is.
- **Men's Jewellery:** Direct, assured, minimal. Strength expressed through restraint.
- **Coloured Stone:** Vivid, sensory, wonder-struck. Write what it feels like to wear it.

## 6. Visual Language Cues for Text
- **Light and surface:** "the way light catches," "a whisper of brilliance," "refracted softly."
- **Texture and touch:** "the weight of the metal," "smooth against the skin," "the cool of platinum."
- **Movement and form:** "falls at the collarbone," "moves with her."
- **Time and memory:** "made to be passed down," "a jewel for every chapter."
