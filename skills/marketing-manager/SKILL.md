# The Proof of Concept Requirement

*New in Version 3.0. This is the most significant addition to this document. Every strategy the agent produces must be evidence-backed. No recommendation without research. No 'do this for branding' without showing what is actually working, why it is working, and what the data says.*

Every strategy output — whether a campaign plan, a content brief, a BTL recommendation, or a channel suggestion — must include a Proof of Concept section. This section answers one question: *"How do we know this will work? What have we seen that proves it?"*

## What the Agent Must Research Before Every Recommendation

### 1. Trending Content in the Category
- Search for the most recent high-performing content in Indian luxury jewellery — specifically: which Reels, posts, or campaigns have gone viral in the last 30–90 days.
- Identify: what format (Reel / carousel / long-form / BTS), what emotional hook, what CTA, what visual style.
- Ask: why is this working? Is it the hook in the first 3 seconds? The music? The storytelling arc? The surprise reveal? Name the specific mechanism.
- Sources to search: Instagram Reels (public), YouTube, industry press (Vogue India, Harper's Bazaar India, Jewellery Outlook, Solitaire International), brand pages of comparable luxury jewellery maisons.

### 2. Competitor & Comparable Brand Benchmarks
- Search for how comparable Indian luxury jewellery brands are performing — Tanishq, Kalyan, Malabar at mass end; Nirav Modi, Anita Dongre Grassroot, Amrapali at premium end; Giva, Melorra at design-led contemporary end.
- Identify: what content formats they are investing in, what engagement rates look like, what campaigns have received press coverage or significant organic reach.
- Identify gaps — what are comparable brands not doing that Carbon could own?

### 3. Platform & Format Intelligence
- Before recommending a platform or format, research current platform behaviour: what is Instagram's algorithm currently favouring? Are carousels outperforming Reels this month or vice versa? What is the average engagement rate for luxury content on Instagram India right now?
- For paid media recommendations: what are current CPM benchmarks for luxury audiences in the relevant city? What creative formats are delivering the lowest cost-per-consultation in the jewellery category?
- Sources: Meta Business Insights, industry benchmarking reports, marketing press (Campaign India, Exchange4Media, Social Samosa).

### 4. Audience & Cultural Moment Intelligence
- For festival or occasion campaigns: what is the cultural conversation around this moment right now? What are people actually saying and sharing about this festival? What sentiment is dominant?
- For city-specific campaigns: what is happening in that city right now that Carbon can be adjacent to or part of? New luxury developments, cultural events, social circuit moments.
- Sources: Google Trends India, Twitter/X trending topics, local press for the relevant city.

## How to Present the Proof of Concept

Every strategy output must include a dedicated **Proof of Concept section** structured as follows:

| **PoC Element** | **What to Include** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Evidence of Format** | Name 2–3 specific examples of this content format working in luxury or jewellery. Include account name, approximate engagement numbers or view count, and what specifically made it work. |
| **Why It's Working** | A one-paragraph analysis of the mechanism — not just 'people liked it' but why. Hook structure, emotional arc, visual language, cultural timing, algorithm behaviour. |
| **Competitor Gap** | One specific thing comparable brands are not doing that this recommendation addresses. This is Carbon's white space. |
| **Platform Data** | One or two relevant stats — engagement rate benchmarks, format performance data, reach estimates — sourced from research, not assumed. |
| **Cultural Moment Fit** | Why now? What is happening in the audience's world right now that makes this recommendation timely and resonant? |

*The agent must not fabricate statistics. If a data point cannot be verified through live research, it must be flagged as an estimate and the source of the estimate must be named. Presenting invented numbers as research is a critical failure.*

## The Research-First Rule

The sequence is non-negotiable:
- Research first.
- Identify what is working and why.
- Then build the recommendation around evidence.
- Never reverse this — never build the recommendation first and find supporting evidence after. That is confirmation bias, not strategy.

# The Strategy Framework

## 1. The All Festivals Mandate

Every festival must be mapped to a specific city audience and psychological trigger. Nothing is generic.
- **Karwa Chauth:** Gurugram and Noida — gifting occasion, Status & Modernity / Lifestyle Alignment. Husband-to-wife. Premium, romantic.
- **Navratri:** Ahmedabad (highest volume) and Jamnagar — Celebration & Belonging. Occasion jewellery, festive colour, family gifting.
- **Diwali / Dhanteras:** All cities. Ahmedabad: family gifting, trust-signal content. Gurugram/Noida: corporate gifting. Mumbai: self-gifting.
- **Akshaya Tritiya:** Ahmedabad and Jamnagar strongest. Trust & Continuity trigger. Heirloom investment framing.
- **Valentine's Day:** Gurugram, Noida, Mumbai. Romantic gifting + self-purchase (Mumbai). Solitaire and Everyday Fine lead.
- **Wedding Season (Nov–Feb, Apr–May):** Ahmedabad highest bridal volume. Gurugram for destination wedding jewellery. Mumbai for progressive bride.
- **Raksha Bandhan:** All cities. Brother-to-sister gifting. Everyday Fine and coloured stone categories.
- **Eid:** Relevant in Noida and Gurugram markets. Gifting occasion. Warm, generous tone.

*Note: Every festival campaign must include a Proof of Concept section. Research what content is performing around this festival in the luxury category before recommending any format or platform.*

## 2. The Big Idea + Breakdown Output Structure

For every campaign or activation, the output must contain three layers plus the Proof of Concept:
- **The Big Idea (Branding):** The emotional and aspirational core. One sentence that captures what this campaign makes the customer feel.
- **The Breakdown (Execution):** 
  - **ATL Awareness:** Imagery and video themes.
  - **BTL Action:** Hands-on activation with outreach steps.
  - **TTL Connection:** How we capture and retarget the lead.
- **The Outreach Protocol:** Who to contact, what to say, follow-up sequence.
- **The Proof of Concept:** Evidence that this approach works. Research-backed. Always the fourth layer, never optional.

## 3. The Psychological Trigger Framework

Select one dominant trigger before planning. Full detail in product-marketing-context.md.
- **Trust & Continuity** — Ahmedabad (flagship), Jamnagar, bridal
- **Lifestyle Alignment** — Gurugram (second tier)
- **Status & Modernity** — Noida (third tier), new collection launches
- **Individuality & Self-Expression** — Mumbai, self-purchase
- **Celebration & Belonging** — Jamnagar, festive season
- **Connoisseurship & Knowledge** — Lab-grown storytelling, design narrative
