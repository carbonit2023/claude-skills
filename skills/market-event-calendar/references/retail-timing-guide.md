*The Phase Architecture: Mapping Human Intent to the Maison Calendar*

## 1. The Intent Matrix (Mapping Story to Speed)

Before setting dates, match the event to the correct **Intent Archetype**. This determines the pace of your campaign.

| **Archetype** | **Intent Layer** | **Timing Rhythm** | **Psychology** | **Maison Voice** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Heirloom Peak** | Layer 1: Retail | Slow Awareness, High Density final 10 days. | Investment & Legacy. | Trust-led, permanent, prestigious. |
| **The Emotional Anchor** | Layer 2: Emotional | Consistent narrative for 3 weeks. No final "rush." | Gratitude & Adoration. | Warm, lyrical, personal. |

## 2. The 4-Phase Architecture (Execution Roadmap)

Every campaign must travel through these distinct mood shifts, including the critical post-event quiet. **You must define the voice change between phases.**

### Phase 1: Awareness (Psychological Pull)
- **Window:** T-6 to T-4 weeks (Flagship) | T-4 to T-3 weeks (Others)
- **Objective:** Seeding the emotion.
- **Mood:** Atmospheric, cinematic, "The whisper."
- **Content:** No products, no prices. Just the **Jewel of Emotion** trigger (e.g., *The feel of a first Navratri*).

### Phase 2: Engagement (Selection & Styling)
- **Window:** T-3 to T-2 weeks
- **Objective:** Curating the desire.
- **Mood:** Informative, aesthetic, "The reveal."
- **Content:** Introducing the collection. Styling guides. Inviting to BTL masterclasses or viewings.

### Phase 3: Conversion (Closing the Loop)
- **Window:** T-1 week to T-0
- **Objective:** Direct Action.
- **Mood:** Direct, concierge-led, "The invitation."
- **Content:** Soft luxury CTAs (Reserve, Speak to Concierge). Final gifting edits.

### Phase 4: The Post-Event Halo (The Gentle Echo)
- **Window:** T+1 to T+14 Days
- **Objective:** Relationship solidification. Capitalizing on high post-event dopamine without the pressure of a deadline.
- **Mood:** Quiet, appreciative, unhurried.
- **Content:** Heritage Circle inductions, "How to care for your new piece," styling the piece for everyday wear. No 'buy' CTAs.

## 3. Micro-Pacing (Day-of-Week Psychology)
A master orchestrator knows that *what* you say matters only if it aligns with *how the customer feels today*. Follow these micro-pacing deployment rules:
- **Sundays & Mondays:** High 'dream state'. Launch **Phase 1 (Awareness)** content here. Long-form video, majestic branding.
- **Tuesdays & Wednesdays:** Active consideration. Deploy **Phase 2 (Engagement)**. Styling carousels, detailed artisan stories.
- **Thursdays & Fridays:** Planning the weekend. Deploy **Phase 3 (Conversion)**. Direct outreach to book weekend in-store viewing appointments.
- **Saturdays:** The Store Visit. Content is paused. Let the physical experience take over.

## 4. Flagship Calibration (The Ahmedabad Rule)

Because **Ahmedabad (Rank 1)** is our flagship, its timing is **different** from the rest of the network:
- **Phase 1 starts 2 weeks earlier:** Legacy wealth buys after consideration, not on impulse. They need more time to "trust" the campaign.
- **Deeper BTL runway:** BTL invitations for Ahmedabad must be personal and sent via direct concierge service at least 15 days in advance.
- **Mood Shift:** Ahmedabad stays in the "Legacy/Trust" mood longer than Noida, which pivots to "Trend/Status" faster.

## 5. Share of Voice (SOV) & Market Noise

You do not map dates in a vacuum. You map them against the noise of the market.
- **The Pre-Surge:** If approaching a high-noise retail festival (e.g., Diwali), start Phase 1 **two weeks earlier** than the entire market to secure mindshare before the noise begins.
- **The Counter-Surge:** If the market is exhausted (e.g., the day after a massive national holiday), do not go quiet. This is the moment for **Quiet Luxury (Layer 3)** visibility.
- **The Fatigue Strike:** Competitors blow 80% of their ad budgets in the first 7 days of their launch. A master timing scout waits until T-minus 5 days—when the competitor's audience is fatigued and their budget is dry—to strike hard with a pristine, high-budget Phase 3 conversion push.
- **Macro-Sentiment Calibration:** If the target city is experiencing economic caution, extend **Phase 1 (Awareness/Trust)** an extra week. Trust takes longer to build in cautious markets.

## 6. Advanced Environmental Triggers (The Master Level)

Beyond dates on a calendar, trigger campaigns based on these three invisible forces:

### A. Liquidity Cycles (Wealth Corridors)
High-Net-Worth buyers don't buy on "payday." They buy during liquidity events:
- **Corporate Bonus Season (March/April):** Critical for Gurugram and Noida (Rank 2 & 3). Trigger "Self-Reward" and "Investment" narratives.
- **NRI Arrival Window (Mid-Dec to Mid-Jan):** Critical for Jamnagar and Ahmedabad. High liquidity, high emotion. Trigger "Heritage & Homecoming" campaigns.
- **Post-Harvest/Agricultural Surplus (October):** Relevant for Tier-1 legacy markets.

### B. Micro-Climates (Sensory Shifts)
Luxury buying is deeply tied to weather.
- **The First Drop in Temperature (Nov):** Triggers winter couture and bridal aspiration in Gurugram/Noida. Launch "Layering & Statement Pieces."
- **Note:** Mumbai does not experience this. Do not run "Winter Styling" in Mumbai; pivot to "Resort/Travel Styling."

### C. Generational Timing Variances
- **Gen-Z / Young Millennials:** They buy *before* the trip or event. Their peak engagement is in the anticipation phase (for Instagram). Peak their timing at T-minus 7.
- **Legacy Buyers (Gen X / Boomers):** They buy *at* or immediately before the occasion. Peak their timing at T-minus 2.

---

## 7. Anti-Pattern Safety Gate

- **NO "Last Minute Sale" timing:** We do not launch "48-hour flash sales."
- **NO "Mother's Day Sale" framing:** Emotional events are for connection. If you time an emotional event as a retail event, you fail the Maison standard.
- **NO Generic Timing:** If you propose a deadline without checking for **local city holidays or clashing events** (e.g. Navratri Garba peak vs Retail Dhanteras), you fail the Proof of Concept mandate.
