## Category A — Experience & Education

### A1. Heirloom Masterclass
- **Concept:** Intimate session (10–15 guests) walking through the design journey—from sketch to hallmarking.
- **Why It Wins:** Builds deep trust through knowledge. Customer leaves understanding the craft, not just the product.
- **Best For:** **Ahmedabad (Flagship Priority)**, Jamnagar.
- **Psychological Trigger:** Trust & Continuity.

### A2. Stone Stories Session
- **Concept:** 90-minute experience around a single stone type (Lab-grown diamonds, Emeralds, or Polki).
- **Why It Wins:** Experiential hook of handling loose stones. Participatory and educational.
- **Best For:** All cities (stone selection adjusted by city profile).
- **Psychological Trigger:** Connoisseurship & Knowledge.

### A4. Design-A-Diamond Atelier (★ New)
- **Concept:** Hands-on sketching session with a Carbon stylist. Customers learn about fancy shapes and proportions by designing their own piece.
- **Why It Wins:** Creative participation is a powerful emotional anchor. 
- **Best For:** Gurugram, Noida, Mumbai.
- **Psychological Trigger:** Individuality & Self-Expression.

## Category B — Social & Lifestyle

### B2. Mixology & Magnificence
- **Concept:** Carbon-inspired cocktails named after collections (e.g., 'The Emerald Haze' for Hues) alongside jewellery storytelling.
- **Why It Wins:** Multi-sensory engagement. High "Instagrammability."
- **Best For:** Gurugram (Rank 2), Noida (Rank 3).
- **Psychological Trigger:** Status & Modernity.

### B5. Designer Lookbook Event (★ New)
- **Concept:** Stylist session showing how to pair Carbon jewellery with modern Indian couture (Sarees/Lehengas).
- **Why It Wins:** Validates Carbon as a design-led fashion authority, not just a retailer.
- **Best For:** **Mumbai (Primary)**, Gurugram (Rank 2).
- **Psychological Trigger:** Individuality & Self-Expression.

## Category C — Content & Community

### C1. The Carbon Collective (Heritage Circle)
- **Concept:** Annual appreciation evening for top-tier clients. No selling—pure gratitude and relationship building.
- **Why It Wins:** The most powerful retention tool. Relationship > Revenue.
- **Best For:** All cities (Scale adapted by city rank).
- **Psychological Trigger:** Belonging & Legacy.

### C3. Festive Gifting Suite
- **Concept:** Dedicated in-store gifting zone set up 3-4 weeks before Diwali/Navratri. Bespoke packaging and concierge delivery.
- **Why It Wins:** Converts a gifting occasion into a brand experience for both the giver and recipient.
- **Best For:** Ahmedabad/Jamnagar (Navratri), Gurugram/Noida (Corporate Gifting).
- **Psychological Trigger:** Celebration & Belonging.

## Activation Selection Framework
When recommending an activation, apply this filter:
1. **City Rank:** Does the format match the location tier (Rank 1–5)?
2. **Psychological Trigger:** Does it activate the right emotional lever for this city?
3. **Budget Realism:** Quality-first. A well-executed Low-budget event beats a bad High-budget one.
4. **Lead Time:** Is there enough runway (2–8 weeks)?
5. **PoC:** Can I cite a real example of this working?
