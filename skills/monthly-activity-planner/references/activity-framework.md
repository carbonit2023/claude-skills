Every activity suggested for Carbon must follow the **Complete Execution Loop**: High Strategy → Practical BTL → Direct Outreach → Evidence-Based Proof. **No step is optional.**

---

## 1. The ATL, BTL & TTL Triad

Every monthly activity must be broken down into all three layers. A plan missing any layer is incomplete.

| Layer | Name | Purpose | Carbon Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ATL** | Above-The-Line | **Awareness.** High-end visual storytelling to create pull. | Brief for Seedance/Freepik. Cinematic, jewellery as hero. No product specs. |
| **BTL** | Below-The-Line | **The Core Activity.** Hands-on, localised, participatory. | No boring lectures. No passive demos. No expensive apparatus. Experiential/sensory. |
| **TTL** | Through-The-Line | **Digital Connection.** Lead capture and retargeting. | QR to WhatsApp concierge. Name, email, occasion data. No lucky draws. |

**The 'No Boring Lectures' rule in BTL is non-negotiable.** If the activity requires the customer to sit and listen for more than 5 minutes without doing something, it is not a Carbon BTL activation. Redesign it.

### The Decision Maker
Identify the specific decision maker — not just the organisation:
- **Residential Society:** Secretary of the Apartment Owners Association (AOA) or Cultural Committee Head.
- **Corporate Office:** HR Head, Admin Manager, or the EA to a senior executive for gifting.
- **Premium F&B / Hotel:** Events Manager or F&B Director — not the front desk.
- **Heritage / Cultural Institution:** Programme Director or Trustee.

### The Elevator Pitch
Reframe Carbon as a gift to the partner's community — not as a brand seeking access:
> *"We aren't here to sell products. We are bringing a curated 'Jewel of Emotion' experience for your residents — an appreciation of fine craftsmanship that they will remember long after the evening. We handle everything. You simply invite your members to something exceptional."*

### The Lead Capture
- **Collect:** First name, email, phone, and the occasion/milestone.
- **Method:** WhatsApp concierge link via QR — conversational, not form-based.
- **What Never to Use:** Lucky draw mechanics, competition entry, or discount redemption.

## 3. Mandatory Output Fields
Every activity plan must contain all seven fields:
1. **Idea Name & Psychological Trigger:** State which of the six triggers it activates and why.
2. **ATL Plan:** Awareness themes, visual direction, briefing notes for Seedance/Freepik.
3. **BTL Plan:** Participatory, sensory activity with No-Bottleneck setup.
4. **TTL Plan:** WhatsApp concierge QR, lead capture protocol, retargeting pixel.
5. **Outreach Guide:** Decision maker identified, contact script, follow-up sequence.
6. **Budget Tier:** Range, reasoning, lead time, and tier-appropriate ceiling check.
7. **Proof of Concept:** Mandatory evidence of format (real examples, data, competitor gap).

## 4. The 7-Step End-to-End Planning Process

| Phase | Name | What Happens | Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0** | **Research** | Live research on trending formats and competitor activity. | **Proof of Concept section** |
| **1** | **Audit** | Review brand context, collection, city profile, and budget. | Context brief |
| **2** | **Theme** | Define the Big Idea — the emotional core. | Big Idea + Named Trigger |
| **3** | **Resource** | Identify venue, partner, collateral, and staffing. | Resource checklist |
| **4** | **Outreach** | Contact decision maker, pitch, and confirm partnership. | Confirmed partner/venue |
| **5** | **Content** | Brief ATL assets, prepare QR, and WhatsApp invites. | Content assets briefed |
| **6** | **Live Prep** | Staff briefing, guest flow design, setup plan. | Day-of checklist |
| **7** | **Retargeting** | Lead data handed to manager. Retargeting activated. | Retargeting brief |

## 5. The No-Bottleneck Rules
These rules exist to prevent over-engineering.
- **No scientific apparatus:** No microscopes or spectrometers. Tell the story through beauty.
- **No budget breaches:** If it exceeds the city tier budget ceiling, redesign it.
- **No passive presentations:** The customer must do something — sketch, touch, taste, wear.
- **No mass walk-ins:** Volume without quality is a brand failure. Curation is mandatory.
- **No discount mechanics:** Not at any touchpoint.
- **No generic venues:** A hotel ballroom is not Carbon. Use heritage properties or boutique studios.
