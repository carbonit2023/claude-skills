# Carbon Media Production Briefing Guide

This reference defines how to brief AI tools (Seedance, Freepik, Nano Banana) for Carbon content.

## 1. Visual Standards (The "Carbon Look")
- **Lighting:** Warm, soft, cinematic.
- **Backgrounds:** Minimalist luxury—marble, silk, high-end architectural textures.
- **Product Focus:** Highlight the "Brilliance" and "Fancy Shapes" (Polki, Emerald cuts).

## 2. Tool-Specific Briefing Patterns
### 📸 For Images (Freepik / Midjourney)
- **Prompt Logic:** "Close up of a fancy-shaped lab-grown diamond ring on a hand of a sophisticated woman in her 30s. Background of a premium lounge in Mumbai. Cinematic lighting, photorealistic, 8k."

### 🎥 For Videos (Seedance / Nano Banana)
- **Prompt Logic:** "Slow motion reveal of the 'Hues' necklace shimmering under event lights. Transition to a premium mixology drink being served. Atmosphere: Exclusive, Aspirational. Resolution: Ultra-HD."

## 3. Mandatory Check for Post-Production
Every media asset must include:
- The **Jewel of Emotion** logo overlay.
- Soft luxury CTAs (Explore, Discover).
- Consistency across the "Hues" or "Bridal" campaign aesthetic.
