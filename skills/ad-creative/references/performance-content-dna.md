# Carbon Performance & Retargeting DNA

This reference guide defines the structured retargeting and performance creative standards for Carbon.

## 1. The Retargeting Engine (The Most Important Part)
Don't just run ads; build a movement from first-click to store-visit.

### Sequence A: The "Brand Relatability" Sequence (TOFU -> MOFU)
- **Target:** Users who engaged with high-level TTL content.
- **Creative:** Focus on the "Design-led" Maison story. Use Seedance/Nano for cinematic reveals.
- **Goal:** Move from "Aspiration" to "Specific Collection Interest."

### Sequence B: The "Abandoned Intent" Sequence (MOFU -> BOFU)
- **Target:** Users who browsed a collection (e.g., Hues) but didn't book an appointment.
- **Creative:** Show local branch activities (e.g., "Join us for a Mixology evening in Noida").
- **Goal:** Drive the store visit invitation.

### Sequence C: The "Post-Visit / Referral" Sequence
- **Target:** Customers who visited or purchased.
- **Creative:** Focus on complementary pieces or "Heirloom" curation.
- **Goal:** Lifetime value and referrals.

## 2. Creative Guidelines for Retargeting
- **Variety:** Switch formats—if they saw a Video first, show a Carousel of certified solitaires next.
- **The "Belonging" Hook:** Use copy that validates their taste. "We saw your appreciation for the 'Hues' palette — here is how we curated it for the Noida Maison."

## 3. SEO-GEO Connection
- Every performance ad must align with the GEO-targeted keywords for that branch (e.g., "Luxury Lab-Grown Diamonds in Gurugram").
